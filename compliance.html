🎯 Key Business Decisions Made by Users

Director's Weekly Decisions
  • Enroll staff: "CPR class expires in 45 days → Book class now"
  • Contact vendor: "Background check renewal → Initiate process"
  • Prepare facility: "DCF inspection in 30 days → Schedule prep"
  • Flag for owner: "Insurance expires in 60 days → Alert owner"
  • Update status: "Compliance item renewed → Mark complete"

  • Start shopping: "Insurance expires in 60 days → Get quotes"
  • Make renewal: "Compare quotes → Select new carrier"
  • Approve costs: "Review renewal costs vs. current"
  • Address violations: "CPR expired → Call director for emergency action"
  • Prepare audit: "Annual audit coming → Export compliance report"

💡 Bottom Line

Compliance Tracking exists to answer ONE question: "What regulatory/contractual items do we need to maintain, and when do they expire?"

Without it: Deadlines get missed, fines are paid, staff can't work, school gets shut down

With it: Owner & Director have 30-60 day advance warning, time to plan, business stays compliant & profitable

Impact on Pulse: Compliance is 15% of Owner's health score. A single expired item can raise pulse from "healthy" (75 BPM) to "stressed" (110 BPM)
